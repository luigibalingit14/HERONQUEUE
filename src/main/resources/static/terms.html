Priority Lane Policy

These institutional regulations govern queue ticketing, priority scheduling, and service verification across all University of Makati student transaction counters. All stakeholders agree to adhere to these operational guidelines.

Effective: Academic Year 2026–2027 • Authority: UMak Student Services & CCIS • System: HeronQueue Virtual Ticketing Engine
1

Priority Lane Mandate & Legal Compliance

In strict compliance with Philippine National Laws—including R.A. 7277 / R.A. 10754 (Magna Carta for Persons with Disabilities) and R.A. 9994 (Expanded Senior Citizens Act)—the HeronQueue Priority Lane is dedicated strictly to:

  • Students with registered and verified disabilities (PWDs).
  • Senior citizens transacting on behalf of academic requirements.
  • Expectant and heavily pregnant students.
  • Individuals with acute medical conditions authorized by University Health Services.
Patakaran sa Priority Lane: Alinsunod sa batas ng Pilipinas, ang Priority Lane ay eksklusibo lamang para sa mga PWD, Senior Citizen, buntis, at mga mag-aaral na may opisyal na medical endorsement mula sa Clinic.
2

Mandatory Credential Presentation & Forfeiture

Possession of a Priority Ticket (e.g., prefix P-###) is an operational reservation in the scheduling queue and does not establish definitive eligibility until verified in person.

Students summoned under a Priority Ticket must present a valid government-issued PWD/Senior ID or an authorized UMak Medical Certificate to the counter staff before transaction processing. Failure to provide qualifying documentation will result in immediate ticket forfeiture. The student will be directed to obtain a new Regular Ticket, joining the queue at its natural conclusion.

Pagpapatunay ng ID: Ang priority ticket ay numero lamang sa pila at hindi patunay ng kwalipikasyon. Kailangang magpakita ng valid PWD/Senior ID sa counter. Kung walang maipakitang ID, kakanselahin ang priority ticket at kailangang kumuha ng Regular ticket.
3

Non-Transferability & Single-Transaction Scope

Each issued queue ticket corresponds exclusively to a single continuous transaction for the designated claimant. Tickets are non-transferable and may not be bartered, assigned, or shared with peers.

A customer who fails to appear after three (3) consecutive public summonses and automated audio callouts shall be flagged as a No-Show, releasing the service counter to subsequent patrons.

Isang Transaksyon Lamang: Bawal ipasa, ibenta, o ipahiram ang ticket sa ibang estudyante. Kapag tinawag ng tatlong beses at hindi lumapit sa counter, mamarkahan ito bilang No-Show.
4

Privacy & Data Protection (R.A. 10173)

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173), HeronQueue adheres to strict privacy-by-design standards:

  • Public displays and monitors broadcast pseudonymous queue tokens only (e.g., P-001, R-005).
  • No names, student identification numbers, or medical particulars are rendered on public screens.
  • System logs maintain strictly necessary technical audit metrics for throughput evaluation and fraud prevention.
Seguridad ng Impormasyon: Alinsunod sa Data Privacy Act, numero lamang ng ticket ang lumalabas sa mga monitor sa lobby upang maprotektahan ang inyong pagkakakilanlan at personal na datos.
5

Anti-Abuse, Rate Limiting & System Governance

The HeronQueue virtual terminal utilizes automated client-side cooldowns (6-second lockout) and server-side rate limiters to preserve queue integrity and forestall double-submissions.

Deliberate ticket hoarding, automated script injection, or disruption of student service operations constitutes a violation of the University of Makati Student Handbook and shall be escalated to the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS).

Anti-Spam at Integridad: May awtomatikong proteksyon ang system laban sa sunod-sunod na pagpindot. Ang sinasadyang pag-abuso o pag-spam ng mga ticket ay ipagbibigay-alam sa pamunuan ng unibersidad alinsunod sa Student Handbook.

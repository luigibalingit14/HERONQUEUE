University of Makati Student Services • Statutory Priority Protocol

Please ensure you meet the institutional criteria:
  • Eligible Patrons: Reserved strictly for Persons with Disabilities (PWD), Senior Citizens, and Expectant/Pregnant students pursuant to R.A. 7277 and R.A. 9994.
  • Mandatory Credential: You must present an official valid PWD ID, Senior Citizen ID, or University Clinic endorsement upon counter summons.
  • Forfeiture Warning: Unverified tickets shall be cancelled immediately, requiring re-entry via the Regular queue.
Paunawa: Ihanda ang inyong valid ID bago lumapit sa counter. Ang ticket na walang kaukulang ID ay awtomatikong kakanselahin alinsunod sa patakaran ng unibersidad.
